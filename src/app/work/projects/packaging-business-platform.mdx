## Overview

Nexon Packaging is a Florida-based custom packaging business selling into the US market - custom
boxes with low minimum order quantities for CBD, candle, skincare, and food brands.

Custom packaging does not fit a standard e-commerce checkout. There is no fixed price on a shelf:
an order starts as a quote request with specifications attached - dimensions, material, finish,
quantity - gets priced by a human, and only then becomes an order. That quote-to-order path is the
whole product.

**Stack:** PHP · Laravel · MySQL

## What I built

### Customer-facing platform

- **Customer management** for B2B accounts, where the buying entity matters more than the
  individual placing the order
- **Quote requests**, capturing the specifications a custom job needs before anyone can price it
- **Order workflows**, moving an accepted quote through to a fulfilled order

### Internal admin panel

The operation also needed somewhere to actually run from, so the platform includes a separate
internal admin application at `admin.nexonpackaging.com`. That is where incoming quote requests get
reviewed and priced, customers and orders are managed, and the business tracks work in progress -
the operational half that customers never see.

## Outcome

I engineered the complete quote-to-order platform for Nexon Packaging, delivering a unified Laravel backend that handles both B2B customer workflows and the internal administrative operations. I owned the architectural design of the core business logic, converting complex, unstructured quote requests involving dimensions, materials, and finishes into structured order pipelines. By building out a dedicated internal admin application, I enabled the business to seamlessly price requests and track work in progress securely. The entire system is backed by a relational MySQL database designed to maintain strict referential integrity across the order lifecycle. The primary trade-off encountered in this domain was the necessity of modelling a highly flexible quotation stage as a first-class workflow, which required far more upfront data design than a traditional e-commerce checkout flow.

**Key facts:**
- Stack: PHP, Laravel, MySQL
- Ownership: Quote-to-order pipeline and internal admin portal
- Focus: Custom B2B workflow architecture and relational data integrity
