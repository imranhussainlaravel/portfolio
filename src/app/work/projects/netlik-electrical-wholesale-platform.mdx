## Overview

[Netlik Light House](https://netliklighthouse.com/) is an established electrical and commercial lighting wholesale distributor based in Lahore, Pakistan (Thokar Niaz Baig). They supply certified commercial LED lighting, industrial switches, distribution switchgear, and pure copper cables nationwide to commercial contractors, developers, and over 250 independent electrical retail shops across Punjab, Sindh, KPK, and Balochistan.

In wholesale electrical distribution, conventional retail shopping carts fall short. Transactions involve high-volume carton and pallet quantities, tiered trade pricing, specialized technical specifications (lumens, color temperature, beam angles, conductor thickness), and contractor-specific bill-of-quantities (BOQ). The business needed a high-performance web platform to showcase their multi-brand catalog, streamline contractor quotation requests, and accelerate order dispatch across Pakistan's commercial hubs.

**Stack:** Next.js (App Router) · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · SQLite / Relational DB · Lucide React

## What I built

### Interactive wholesale catalog and multi-brand hub

- **Categorized Discovery:** Built structured browsing across core product lines: slim ceiling panels, heavy-duty IP66 floodlights, LED bulbs, commercial COB track lights, LED batten tubes, pure copper cables, and distribution switchgear.
- **Brand Showcase:** Dedicated brand hubs for certified manufacturers including Pakistan Cables, Schneider Electric, Havells, Philips, Osaka, and Pak Lite, maintaining brand integrity and technical transparency.
- **Fast Client-Side Filtering:** Implemented instant filtering by product category, brand, power wattage, and technical ratings, enabling trade buyers to find exact equipment specifications in seconds.
- **Trade Terms & Wholesale Info:** Clear presentation of minimum order quantities (MOQ), volume tiers, nationwide cargo forwarding, and trade replacement warranty policies.

### Multi-step quote wizard

- **Structured BOQ Ingestion:** Designed and built an intuitive multi-step wizard allowing electrical contractors and purchasing agents to input custom project requirements, required quantities, and project delivery locations.
- **Specification Gathering:** Captures exact electrical tolerances, brand preferences, and installation timelines so sales engineers receive complete information without back-and-forth phone tag.
- **Quotation Workflow:** Routes requests directly to the commercial sales team for fast, accurate pricing turnaround.

### Trade basket and WhatsApp dispatch engine

- **Persistent Trade Basket:** Engineered a client-side trade basket allowing buyers to compile products across multiple brands and categories into a unified list of requirements.
- **Direct WhatsApp Order Generation:** Built automated workflow integration that formats basket items into a structured, readable message and opens direct communication with Netlik sales desks via WhatsApp.
- **Zero-Friction Checkout:** Eliminates account barriers for trade buyers on mobile devices, matching how electrical commerce actually moves in the local market.

### Back-office admin and media management

- **Catalog & Inventory Control:** Built an administrative interface to manage products, update technical specifications, adjust pricing tiers, and configure featured categories.
- **In-Browser Image Cropper:** Developed custom client-side image cropping tools (`ProductImageCropper` and `BrandLogoCropper`) ensuring vendor product photos and manufacturer logos render with uniform aspect ratios across the catalog.
- **SQLite Database Architecture:** Designed lightweight, performant data models to store product hierarchies, brand relationships, and system configuration.

### Custom interactive theme engine

- **Trade & Night Mode:** Integrated a custom interactive bulb toggle (`ThemeBulb`) allowing users to seamlessly transition between high-contrast daylight trade mode and luxury dark mode, preserving visual clarity in all contractor environments.

## Outcome

I engineered the complete wholesale web platform for Netlik Light House, delivering a modern Next.js application tailored to the realities of commercial electrical trade in Pakistan. By replacing static PDF price sheets with an interactive catalog and dynamic quote wizard, the platform significantly reduced quoting friction and inquiries turnaround time. The WhatsApp dispatch integration provided immediate trade conversion for contractors on job sites, helping expand Netlik's wholesale distribution reach nationwide.
