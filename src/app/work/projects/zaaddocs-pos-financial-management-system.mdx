## Overview

ZaadDOCS is a business management and point-of-sale platform. It covers the full commercial cycle -
customers, suppliers, agents, products, sales, purchases, payments, ledgers, invoices, and
expenses - and rolls all of it up into financial reporting.

**Stack:** Laravel · PHP · MySQL · Blade

## What I built

### Database design

I designed the database. In a system where sales, purchases, payments, and expenses all post into
ledgers, the schema *is* the business logic - get the relationships and constraints wrong and every
report downstream is wrong with them. Transactions and referential integrity carry real weight here,
because a partially-applied payment is worse than a rejected one.

### Core backend and financial logic

I developed the core backend and the financial business logic sitting on top of that schema: how
sales and purchases post, how payments settle against invoices, how agent activity is attributed,
and how ledger balances derive from the underlying entries rather than being stored and drifted.

### POS interfaces and reporting

I built the Blade-based POS interfaces - server-rendered, because a point-of-sale screen needs to be
fast and dependable rather than clever - along with daily and monthly financial reporting.

## Outcome

The system handles day-to-day retail operations and closes the loop into financial reporting, so the
same entries that record a sale at the counter are the ones that produce the monthly figures.
